# Finding your way

Drums Practice takes you from your first beat to pro, one step a day. This page shows you how to
get around: the first launch, the list, the keys, the one button that always knows what comes
next, and where everything else lives. Piano Practice is laid out the same way, so once you know
one you know the other.

You only need four keys to start: **JOG** (turn the wheel to move, press it to **CLICK**),
**BACK** and **MENU**. Everything else is named on screen when you need it.

## Your first launch

The first time you open the module it says hello, in three short pages. Turn the jog or click to
go on.

1. **Welcome** — what the module is, and one rule worth knowing: *lit pads can be pressed.*
2. **Keys** — the five you will use most:

   ```
   JOG    move
   CLICK  open
   BACK   up one level
   MENU   help, anywhere
   SHIFT+CLICK  options
   ```

3. **New to drums?** — turn the jog to pick, click to go on:
   - **From the start** puts the path at its first stage and the cursor on **Continue**. Click it
     and your first lesson begins.
   - **I already play** opens the **Path** with a tip: *Know a stage already? SHIFT+CLICK a stage,
     then Test out: checkpoint. Pass it and the path moves on.* See
     [Skipping what you already know](#skipping-what-you-already-know) below.

**Back** on any page ends the welcome; it will not come back on its own. If you practised before
this version, you are not greeted at all — you know your way already. To see it again: *Settings ›
Help › Welcome again*.

## The list

Everything starts from one list. It shows four rows at a time, with the keys under them:

```
 DRUMS
 Continue     Quarters        <- the next step, named
 Path                >
 Practice            >
 Quiz                >
     CLICK open  MENU help    <- the hint line
```

Turn the jog past Quiz and two more rows come into view:

| Row | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue** | the one button: opens the next step for you (below) |
| **Path** | the course: seven stages from *0 Hello, kit* to *6 Pro*, then **Review** while something is due |
| **Practice** | the free library: **Subdivisions**, **Grooves** by style, **Rudiments** by family, **Random** |
| **Quiz** | five ear and reading drills |
| **Progress** | how far you have got: overall, per track, and every drill you played |
| **Settings** | **Path**, **Practice**, **Sound**, **Pads**, **MIDI**, **Help** |

**Click** opens a row, **Back** goes up one level, and Back at the top closes the module. Inside a
folder the hint line says so: `CLICK open  BACK up`.

## The hint line, and what Shift does

The bottom line of every screen names the keys that work there, in the Move's own words:

| Screen | Says |
| --- | --- |
| the list | `CLICK open  MENU help` |
| a folder | `CLICK open  BACK up` |
| a drill, ready | `REC start`, then `PLAY listen  BACK up` |
| a take, paused | `REC go on  BACK stop` |
| a result | `CLICK next  REC again` |
| a lesson card | `JOG page  CLICK next` |
| options | `CLICK do it`, then `BACK close` |

When there is more than fits on one line, it turns over every three seconds — wait a moment and
the rest appears. **Hold SHIFT** and the line shows what Shift does on that screen instead:
`SHIFT: CLICK options`, `SHIFT: BACK quit`.

While a drill is playing, the bottom line is the bar that fills as you go — pause, and the keys are
back.

## Continue: your one button

**Continue** is the top row, and the row says what it will open: `Continue   Quarters`. Click it and
you are in that step — its lesson card first if it is new, then the drill, set to the speed the step
asks for. When you are done, **Click** on the result goes on to the next step.

What you have learned comes back now and then to keep it fresh. When something is due, the row says
so — `Review   Flam tap` — and Continue alternates: one review, then one new step, then a review
again. Everything due today is also in **Path › Review**.

Example: a first session.

1. Click **Continue** — `0 Hello, kit` introduces itself on a card. Click.
2. Read the next card or not — click starts the practice either way.
3. **REC** plays the drill and scores you; **PLAY** lets you listen first.
4. On the result, **Click** goes on to the next step. **Back** goes back to the list.

## The Path

Open **Path** and you see the seven stages. The one you are in is marked `▸`, a finished one ✓, and
a stage you have played in shows `3/12` — steps learned out of all.

Open a stage and its places are listed in the order a lesson takes them:

```
 1 EIGHTH NOTES
 Lessons         read this stage's cards again
 Ear             hear it first
 Time            the pulse, reading, the Clock
 Hands           hand patterns, rudiments, dynamics
 Grooves         beats rung by rung, fills, phrases
 Electives       more of the same idea, never required
 Checkpoint      the piece that proves the stage
```

Nothing is locked. Every step can be opened at any time, and a take anywhere counts — play a flam
tap from Practice › Rudiments and the step that asks for it is done.

## The action sheet: options for a row

**Hold SHIFT and click** a row of the list for its options. Only the ones that fit the row are
shown, and **Settings...** is always last:

| Option | Shown on | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I know this: skip** | a step, or Continue | passes the step by; it still counts if you play it later |
| **I know all this: skip** | a stage, a track, a groove | skips everything open inside it |
| **Undo skip** | anything skipped | takes the skips back |
| **Start the path here** | a stage | Continue starts there from now on |
| **Test out: checkpoint** | a stage | plays the stage's checkpoint |
| **Lessons** | a stage with cards | its lesson cards |
| **Practise: Ladder** | a drill with a tempo | climbs from 70% of its tempo, a rung at a time |
| **Practise: Clock** | a drill with a tempo | the click drops out and you keep the time |
| **Settings...** | every row | the Settings folder; Back comes back here |

Turn the jog to choose, **CLICK** to do it, **BACK** to close the sheet without doing anything.

### Skipping what you already know

You can play already? Test out instead of working through what you know:

1. Open **Path**, turn to the stage you think you know, and **SHIFT+CLICK** it.
2. Choose **Test out: checkpoint**. The stage's checkpoint opens — a piece of music, not a list.
3. Play it clean. The stage is through, and Continue moves on to the next one.

Just one step in your way? **SHIFT+CLICK Continue** and choose **I know this: skip**.

### The Ladder and the Clock

They are not rows of their own: they wrap a drill. Open a groove's rungs (Practice › Grooves ›
Rock & Pop › Rock backbeat), turn to **L1**, **SHIFT+CLICK**, and choose **Practise: Ladder** or
**Practise: Clock**. L0 has neither — it has no clock to climb or to take away.

## MENU: help, anywhere

Press **MENU** on any screen and it explains that screen: what it is, its keys — the same ones as
its hint line, and Shift's — and the help that belongs to it. On a row of the Path it adds that
stage's or step's lesson card, so the *why* of a step is one press away. Turn the jog to scroll,
**BACK** or **MENU** to close. In a drill, the take pauses while you read.

## Tips

The first time you open a sheet, a drill and a quiz, a short tip appears — one card, once:

- *Options for this row. JOG choose, CLICK do it, BACK close.*
- *REC plays and scores it, PLAY lets you listen first. MENU: help.*
- *Answer with the lit pads or the jog. MENU: help.*

Click or Back and it is gone. *Settings › Help › Show tips again* brings them all back.

## Settings

**Settings** is the last row of the list, a folder of groups:

| Group | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Path** | what Continue offers: Tracks and Styles on or off, Rungs `full` or `short`, Start at, Intros |
| **Practice** | tempo, speed, reading, how strictly you are judged, Study, Guide pads, the Ladder and the Clock |
| **Sound** | the click, and latency |
| **Pads** | the pad layout, Flip, and the Pad map |
| **MIDI** | where your hits go, and on which channel |
| **Help** | Show tips again, Welcome again |

Open a group, click a row, turn the jog to change it, click again when done. In a drill, **SHIFT +
CLICK** opens every setting at once over the drill, and the same again takes you back to it.

## Lost?

Press **MENU**. It always tells you where you are and which keys work. **BACK** always goes one
level up — press it a few times and you are back at the top of the list, where **Continue** knows
what to do next.
