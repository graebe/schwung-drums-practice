# Finding your way

Drums Practice takes you from your first beat to pro, one step a day. This page shows you how to
get around: the first launch, the list, the keys, the one button that always knows what comes
next, and where everything else lives. Piano Practice is laid out the same way, so once you know
one you know the other.

You only need four keys to start: **JOG** (turn the wheel to move, press it to **CLICK**),
**BACK** and **MENU**. Everything else is named on screen when you need it.

## Your first launch

The first time you open the module it says hello, in a few short pages. Turn the jog or click to
go on.

1. **Welcome** (two screens) — what the module is, and one rule worth knowing: *lit pads can be
   pressed.*
2. **Keys** — the five you will use most:

   ```
   JOG    move
   CLICK  open
   BACK   up one level
   MENU   help, anywhere
   SHIFT+CLICK  options
   ```

3. **New to drums?** — turn the jog to pick, click to go on:
   - **From the start** puts the path at its first stage and opens the **Learning Path**'s map, your
     avatar on the first island. Press **PLAY** and your first lesson begins.
   - **I already play** opens the **Learning Path** — the map — with a tip: *Know a stage already? SHIFT+CLICK a stage,
     then Test out: checkpoint. Pass it and the path moves on.* See
     [Skipping what you already know](#skipping-what-you-already-know) below.

**Back** on any page ends the welcome; it will not come back on its own. If you practised before
this version, you are not greeted at all — you know your way already. To see it again: *Settings ›
Help › Welcome again*.

## Home

Everything starts from home: five circles, one per section, each with its picture.

```
 DRUMS
  (flag) (drum) ( ? ) (bars) (gear)    <- the chosen one filled and bigger
             Learning Path             <- its name
             JOG choose                <- the hint line
```

**Turn the jog** to choose a circle — it grows as you land on it — and **CLICK** to open it. The
five pads under the circles, on the bottom row, are lit: press one to open its section at once.
Pads make no sound here. **Back** at home closes the module; **Back** from a section comes home
with that circle chosen. **SHIFT+CLICK** and *Show as list* gives the same five as rows, until you
Back out of it.

| Circle | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Learning Path** | the course, as a map: seven stages from *0 Hello, kit* to *6 Pro* (see [The map](#the-map)); PLAY there is Continue |
| **Practice** | the free library: **Subdivisions**, **Grooves** by style, **Rudiments** by family, **Random** |
| **Quiz** | five ear and reading drills |
| **Progress** | how far you have got: overall, per track, and every drill you played |
| **Settings** | **Learning Path**, **Practice**, **Sound**, **Pads**, **MIDI**, **Help** |

Inside a section everything is a list, four rows at a time with the keys under them: **Click**
opens a row, **Back** goes up one level. The hint line says so: `CLICK open  BACK up`.

## The hint line, and what Shift does

The bottom line of every screen names the keys that work there, in the Move's own words:

| Screen | Says |
| --- | --- |
| home | `JOG choose`, then `CLICK open  PAD open`, `MENU help` |
| a folder | `CLICK open  BACK up` |
| a drill, ready | the level at the left — `L2  + kick` — and `JOG level`, `REC start`, … at the right |
| a take, paused | `REC go on  BACK stop` |
| a result | `CLICK next  REC again` |
| a lesson card | `JOG page  CLICK next` |
| options | `CLICK do it`, then `BACK close` |

When there is more than fits on one line, it turns over every three seconds — wait a moment and
the rest appears. **Hold SHIFT** and the line shows what Shift does on that screen instead:
`SHIFT: CLICK options`, `SHIFT: BACK quit`.

While a drill is playing, the bottom line is the bar that fills as you go — pause, and the keys are
back.

## Continue: your one button

**Continue** lives in the Learning Path: on its map it is **PLAY**, and in the Learning Path as a list it is the
first row, which says what it will open: `Continue   Quarters`. A long name that does not fit shows
only its rung — `Continue   L0` for *First beat L0* — and the title bar names the whole step while
the cursor is on the row: `FIRST BEAT L0`. Press it and
you are in that step — its lesson card first if it is new, then the drill, set to the speed the step
asks for. When you are done, **Click** on the result goes on to the next step.

What you have learned comes back now and then to keep it fresh. When something is due, the row says
so — `Review   Flam tap` — and Continue alternates: one review, then one new step, then a review
again. Everything due today is also in **Learning Path › Review**.

Example: a first session.

1. Open **Learning Path** and press **PLAY** (Continue) — `0 Hello, kit` introduces itself on a card. Click.
2. **Meet the pads**: each drum of your layout in turn, its name on screen, its pads pulsing on both
   halves, its sound once. Strike a pulsing pad to meet the next; another pad is only named, the jog
   skips, **PLAY** sounds it again, **Back** leaves without counting it. At the end, **Click** goes on.
3. Read the next card or not — click starts the practice either way.
4. **REC** plays the drill and scores you; **PLAY** lets you listen first.
5. On the result, **Click** goes on to the next step. **Back** goes back to the list.

## The Learning Path

Open **Learning Path** and you see the seven stages. The one you are in is marked `▸`, a finished one ✓, and
a stage you have played in shows `3/12` — steps learned out of all.

Open a stage and its places are listed in the order a lesson takes them:

```
 1 EIGHTH NOTES
 Lessons         read this stage's cards again
 Ear             hear it first
 Time            the pulse, reading, the Clock
 Hands           hand patterns, rudiments, dynamics
 Grooves         beats rung by rung, fills, phrases
 Electives       more of the same idea, never required
 Checkpoint      the piece that proves the stage
```

Nothing is locked. Every step can be opened at any time, and a take anywhere counts — play a flam
tap from Practice › Rudiments and the step that asks for it is done.

## The map

**Learning Path** opens as a map: the sea, with an island for each stage and a dotted route between them.
The screen is the pad grid — each island sits right above its pad — and the pads of the places are
lit, so *lit means you can go there*:

| Pad colour | Means |
| --- | --- |
| green | done |
| white, pulsing | where Continue is taking you |
| dim yellow | started |
| dim | not yet begun, or still ahead |
| blue | skipped, or assumed known |
| bright white | where you stand |

**Press a lit pad** and your avatar walks there along the route; press it again to go in. The jog
walks too — one place a click — and **CLICK** goes in. **BACK** goes up a level, and the avatar is
back on the island you came from. **PLAY** is Continue. On the map the pads make no sound; in a
list they are the kit again.

The title bar names the place you stand on and how much of it is learned — `1 Eighth notes  12%` —
and how many reviews are due there when it fits: `Time  33% 2 due`. On the map, an island's ground
rises as you learn it, a flag flies on a finished one, a dotted outline is a stage you skipped, and
a small dot says reviews are due.

Go in — the map zooms in on the island — and the stage is one island with its places built on it,
the footpath looping round it in the order a lesson takes them:

| Building | Is |
| --- | --- |
| signpost | the stage's **Lessons**, where it starts |
| bell tower | **Ear** |
| clock tower | **Time** |
| workshop | **Hands** |
| festival stage | **Grooves** |
| cove, with its chest | the **Electives** |
| fort | the **Checkpoint** |

A building's walls fill as you learn what is in it, and a flag goes up on its roof when it is done.
Going into a place opens its list, exactly as before — the signpost opens the cards, the fort plays
the checkpoint. BACK zooms out to the world again.

**The path moves.** When CLICK on a result goes on along the path and you have finished something,
you see it before the next step opens. Finish a place — a track's last step — and its flag goes
up, then the avatar walks to the place of the next step. Finish a stage and fireworks go off over
it, a banner says **STAGE COMPLETE** (or **CHECKPOINT PASSED**), the map zooms out, the avatar sails
to the next island, the map zooms in and the avatar walks to the next step. The whole path through
ends with **LEARNING PATH COMPLETE** and fireworks over every island. A level learned for the first
time shows its result's number on its own, rays round it and **LEVEL LEARNED** under it, then the result. Each is
said aloud, and **any key or pad skips it** to where it was going; nothing sounds and nothing is
recorded meanwhile.

**The list is one option away.** SHIFT+CLICK on the map and choose **Show as list**: the folder is
a list again until you leave it, and BACK returns to the map. That is also where **Review** is, the
row after the stages while something is due — it is no island. **MENU** on the map explains it, with
the lesson card of the place you stand on.

Example: to go straight to the Grooves of stage 2 — open **Learning Path**, press the pad lit under island 2,
press it again, then press the Grooves place's pad twice.

## The action sheet: options for a row

**Hold SHIFT and click** a row of the list for its options. Only the ones that fit the row are
shown, and **Settings...** is always last:

| Option | Shown on | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I know this: skip** | a step, or Continue | passes the step by; it still counts if you play it later |
| **I know it all: skip** | a stage, a track, a groove | skips everything open inside it |
| **Undo skip** | anything skipped | takes the skips back |
| **Start the path here** | a stage | Continue starts there from now on |
| **Test out: checkpoint** | a stage | plays the stage's checkpoint |
| **Lessons** | a stage with cards | its lesson cards |
| **Practise: Ladder** | a drill with a tempo | climbs from 70% of its tempo, a rung at a time |
| **Practise: Clock** | a drill with a tempo | the click drops out and you keep the time |
| **Show as list** / **Show as map** | the Learning Path and its stages | the map's folder as a list, and back |
| **Settings...** | every row | the Settings folder; Back comes back here |

Turn the jog to choose, **CLICK** to do it, **BACK** to close the sheet without doing anything.

### Skipping what you already know

You can play already? Test out instead of working through what you know:

1. Open **Learning Path**, turn to the stage you think you know, and **SHIFT+CLICK** it.
2. Choose **Test out: checkpoint**. The stage's checkpoint opens — a piece of music, not a list.
3. Play it clean. The stage is through, and Continue moves on to the next one.

Just one step in your way? Show the Learning Path as a list, **SHIFT+CLICK Continue** and choose **I know
this: skip**.

### The Ladder and the Clock

They are not rows of their own: they wrap a drill. Open a groove's rungs (Practice › Grooves ›
Rock & Pop › Rock backbeat), turn to **L1**, **SHIFT+CLICK**, and choose **Practise: Ladder** or
**Practise: Clock**. L0 has neither — it has no clock to climb or to take away.

## A groove's level

A groove — Rock backbeat, First beat — is one row. Open it and you are on its ready screen, with
the controls on the left of the box and its **LEVEL** on the right: **L0** at your own pace, then
**L1** cymbals only, **L2** + kick, **L3** + snare, **L4** everything. **Turn the jog** to change
it; the arrows show where you can still go, and a tick that the level is learned. A groove opens on
the first level you have not learned. On the Learning Path the jog walks only the levels the stage asks for.
**Click** on a result goes on to the next level.

## MENU: help, anywhere

Press **MENU** on any screen and it explains that screen first: what it is, its keys — the same
ones as its hint line, and Shift's. Under a *More help* line follows the manual's help that belongs
to it; the count in the corner (`1/40`) says how far there is to scroll. On a row of the Learning Path it adds that
stage's or step's lesson card, so the *why* of a step is one press away. Turn the jog to scroll,
**BACK** or **MENU** to close. In a drill, the take pauses while you read.

## Tips

The first time you open a sheet, a drill, a quiz, the map and an island, a short tip appears — one card, once:

- *Options for this row. JOG choose, CLICK do it, BACK close.*
- *REC plays and scores it, PLAY lets you listen first. MENU: help.*
- *Answer with the lit pads or the jog. MENU: help.*
- on the map: *Lit pads are places. Press one to walk there, again to go in. PLAY continues.*
- on the first island: *Each place is part of the stage. The fort is its checkpoint, the signpost
  its lessons.*

Click or Back and it is gone. *Settings › Help › Show tips again* brings them all back.

## Settings

**Settings** is the last row of the list, a folder of groups:

| Group | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Learning Path** | what Continue offers: Tracks and Styles on or off, Rungs `full` or `short`, Start at, Intros |
| **Practice** | tempo, speed, reading, how strictly you are judged, Study, Guide pads, the Ladder and the Clock |
| **Sound** | the click, and latency |
| **Pads** | the pad layout, Flip, and the Pad map |
| **MIDI** | where your hits go, and on which channel |
| **Help** | Show tips again, Welcome again |

Open a group, click a row, turn the jog to change it, click again when done. In a drill, **SHIFT +
CLICK** opens every setting at once over the drill, and the same again takes you back to it.

## Lost?

Press **MENU**. It always tells you where you are and which keys work. **BACK** always goes one
level up — press it a few times and you are home; open **Learning Path** and **PLAY** knows what to do
next.
